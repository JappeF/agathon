# Help

Agathon is a helper and sounding board for Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary: your character sheet, a library of spells, and a place to work spell ideas out. The dice stay on the table, and so do the decisions: nothing here enforces a rule, the Storyteller has the last word.

Everything you make stays on this device. Nothing is sent to a server unless you share it yourself.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

- **Make a character**: type a name under Characters and tap Add. Or tap **Import** and pick your filled-in Mr. Gone M20 2-page sheet (the fillable PDF), or an Agathon character file someone sent you.
- **The character in play** is the one in the bar (bottom right on a phone, top right on a computer). The library, spell ideas and Copy for AI all work with that character. Tap the arrow beside the portrait to switch, add or import.
- **Install it**: in your browser's menu choose Install app or Add to home screen. It then opens like any app and works offline.

## The character sheet {#sheet}

- The sheet opens in **play mode**: dots are locked so nothing changes by accident, while the things that change during play (Health, Willpower, Quintessence, Paradox, XP) stay tappable.
- Tap **Edit** to change the rest, and **Done** when you are finished. Changes are saved as you go.
- In edit mode, fields like Concept, Affiliation, Sect, Nature, Backgrounds, Merits, Flaws, Other traits and the Focus have a pick list from M20 with a short hint and the page. Your own names always work too.
- Tap a dot to set a rating; tap the top dot again to lower it by one.
- Under a Sphere you can note a specialty and tick the affinity Sphere.
- **Show Abilities at 0** (Traits tab) also lists the Abilities you have no dots in.
- The **...** menu shares, copies or deletes the character, and exports or updates the PDF sheet.

## Health, Willpower and the wheel {#trackers}

- The pills under the name show how you are doing. Tap them to open Health and Willpower; in edit mode they open by themselves.
- **Health**: tap a circle to cycle through bashing (one stroke), lethal (a cross), aggravated (a star) and empty. The colour of the status runs from green (Healthy) to red as the wound penalty grows.
- **Willpower**: the dots are your permanent rating (set in edit mode), the circles below are the points you can spend. Tap a circle to mark it spent.
- **Quintessence and Paradox** share one wheel of 20, as on the sheet (M20 p. 331): Quintessence fills clockwise from the dot, Paradox counterclockwise. Where they meet, Paradox wins.

## Your Mr. Gone PDF sheet {#pdf}

- **Import** reads every field of Mr. Gone's fillable M20 2-page sheet, both the older and the newer version.
- Changed the PDF outside the app? **Update from PDF** (in the sheet's ... menu) shows what would change before anything does.
- **Export PDF sheet** fills a copy of the sheet with your character, portrait included, and downloads it. It stays editable in Acrobat Reader and other PDF apps.
- A character made in the app needs a blank sheet for that: the first export fetches Mr. Gone's current one. Offline, the app asks you to pick it yourself and shows where to download it.

## The library {#library}

- Over 500 spells from M20 and How Do You DO That?, each in our own short words with the book page. The rules themselves are in the books.
- **The Sphere circles** filter by rank: tap a Sphere to search up to rank 1, again for 2, and so on. The ring around the circle shows the rank.
- **What ... can cast** shows only what the character in play has the dots for, and what is one dot away. **Only ... favorites** shows the starred spells.
- A spell with several ways to cast it lists them all, with the dots you are short.
- Tap the star to make a spell a favorite (it shows on the sheet), or **Start a spell idea from this** to work a version of it out.

## Searching {#searching}

Type words to search names and descriptions; every word must match. Put a phrase in "quotes", and start a word with a minus to leave it out (`-fire`).

| Type | Finds |
| --- | --- |
| `mind:3` | Mind up to rank 3 |
| `mind:=3` | Mind at exactly rank 3 |
| `mind:3+` | Mind at rank 3 or more |
| `mind:2-4` | Mind from rank 2 to 4 |
| `mind:3 forces:2` | exactly this combination of Spheres (tick Allow other Spheres too for more) |
| `uses:mind,forces` | these Spheres, others allowed |
| `tag:healing` | spells with this tag (the list in the sidebar) |
| `book:hdydt` or `book:m20` | spells from one book |
| `kind:rote`, `kind:feat`, `kind:rank` | rotes, single effects, or what a Sphere rank can do |
| `is:vulgar`, `is:coincidental` | how it usually looks to Sleepers |
| `is:fav` | the character's favorites |
| `name:portal` | only in the name |

Every operator can be turned around with a minus: `-time:`, `-tag:combat`. Technocratic names work as well: `data:` for Correspondence, `primal:` for Prime, `dimsci:` for Spirit.

## Spell ideas {#spell-ideas}

- A spell idea is a dossier for something your mage wants to pull off: what should happen, the library spells it builds on, and one or more versions of the plan.
- In a version, tap the Spheres it takes; tap again to raise the rank. The app shows the difficulty (coincidental, vulgar, with witnesses) and whether the character has the dots.
- **Try another way** starts a new version; older ones stay below and can become the plan again.
- **At the table** keeps a log: how it went (worked, partly, failed, botched), the Paradox it cost and a note.
- **Your rulebooks**: add your own M20 or How Do You DO That? PDF under Spell ideas and the book's own pages can go along to an AI. The text stays on this device.

## Working a spell out with an AI {#ai}

- **Copy for AI** copies everything an AI needs: the character's Arete, Spheres and focus, the idea and its plan, the linked spells and the rules. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini and ask away.
- Pages from your rulebooks make the prompt long. Untick them, or use **All pages** to switch them all on or off at once; the preview shows the size. Cheaper AI models prefer short prompts.
- **Paste the answer** reads the plan back in as a new version. When the AI suggests a better name or goal, each comes with a tick box, so you choose what to take.

## Sharing and backups {#sharing}

- **Share character** (in the sheet's ... menu) gives a file, a link or a QR code. Notes stay private unless you tick them. Tick **Include spell ideas** to send the character's ideas along.
- A **spell idea** has its own share button: the link shows its title and goal in chat apps, and opens in Agathon to add it to one of your characters.
- A **library spell** can be shared from its detail page too.
- **Back up all characters** (in the character menu) saves one file with all characters and their spell ideas. Importing it later adds them back, each idea with its own character.
- Imports only ever add: nothing gets overwritten, and a name that already exists gets a number.
- Your browser can clear site data on its own, especially on phones. Back up now and then.

## Experience {#experience}

For reference only: the app does not count or check experience. XP is a field you fill in yourself, and how it is spent is up to your table.

The costs from M20 (p. 253 and p. 336), where "current" is the rating you have now:

| Raising | Costs |
| --- | --- |
| A new Ability | 3 |
| A new Sphere | 10 |
| The affinity Sphere | current x 7 |
| Another Sphere | current x 8 |
| Arete | current x 8 |
| An Attribute | current x 4 |
| An Ability | current x 2 |
| A Background | current x 3 (current x 6 for one that costs double) |
| Willpower | current x 1 |

An example: Forces is your affinity Sphere at 2. Raising it to 3 costs 2 x 7 = 14 XP. Raising Arete from 3 to 4 costs 3 x 8 = 24 XP.

## Offline and updates {#offline}

- After the first visit the whole app works offline: the sheet, the library, spell ideas and your rulebooks. Only fetching the blank PDF sheet and opening share link previews need a connection.
- When there is a new version, a message offers to reload. Your characters stay where they are.
